# Meeting Recorder

Records meeting audio (Zoom, Teams, Meet) and your microphone on separate channels, in the browser, without the meeting app's own recording.

Open the GitHub Pages link in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

- Windows: share "Entire screen" and turn on "Also share system audio".
- Mac: join the meeting in a Chrome tab and share that tab with "Also share tab audio".

Stop and save puts the recording (.webm) and an attendee list (.txt) in your Downloads. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
