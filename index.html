Meeting Recorder

Records the whole conversation, your voice and everyone else's, into one MP3 on your computer. It works without Zoom, Teams or Meet's own recording. Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer.

Where is the meeting?
How it works
  1. For online calls, wear headphones so the call doesn't echo into your mic.
  2. Press Start recording and allow the microphone.
  3. For online calls a share window opens. Windows: pick "Entire screen" and turn on "Also share system audio". This works with the Zoom or Teams desktop app. Mac: join the meeting in a Chrome tab (Meet, or the Zoom or Teams web version), pick that tab, and turn on "Also share tab audio".
  4. Ask everyone to say their name once at the start ("Hi, this is Priya"). This is what lets the transcript put the right name on each voice.
  5. Press Stop and save when the meeting ends. You get an MP3 and a notes file. Upload both to the project thread for the transcript and minutes.
