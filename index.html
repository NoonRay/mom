Meeting Recorder

Records the meeting audio and your microphone on separate channels, on your own computer, without using Zoom, Teams or Meet recording. Open this file in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

  1. Wear headphones so the call doesn't echo into your mic.
  2. Press Start recording and allow the microphone.
  3. A share window opens. Windows: pick "Entire screen" and turn on "Also share system audio". This works with the Zoom or Teams desktop app. Mac: join the meeting in a Chrome tab (Meet, or the Zoom or Teams web version), pick that tab, and turn on "Also share tab audio".
  4. Ask everyone to say their name once at the start.
  5. Press Stop and save when the meeting ends, then upload the saved files to the project thread.
